About FeberCare

FeberCare is an educational mobile application designed to help parents and caregivers calculate fever medicine doses based on a child’s weight and organize daily dose schedules.

FeberCare does not provide medical advice and does not replace consultation with a doctor or pharmacist.

Key Features

  • Weight-based dose calculation
  • Supports multiple children profiles
  • Clear dose display (mg, ml, spoon equivalents)
  • Daily dose schedule
  • Works offline
  • Supports Arabic, English, and Swedish
  • No data collection or tracking
